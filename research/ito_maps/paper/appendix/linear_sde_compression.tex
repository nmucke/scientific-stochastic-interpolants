\section{Compression in a Linear SDE}\label{app:compression}
For $dX_r=-X_r\,dr+dW_r$, fixed $X_0=0$, and $0=r_0<\cdots<r_K=T$, let $Z_j=W_{r_{j+1}}-W_{r_j}$. Independent Gaussian integration gives
\begin{align}
 \operatorname{Var}(X_T\mid Z)
 ={}&\int_0^T e^{-2(T-r)}dr\nonumber\\
 &-\sum_{j=0}^{K-1}
 \frac{\left(\int_{r_j}^{r_{j+1}}e^{-(T-r)}dr\right)^2}
 {r_{j+1}-r_j}.
\end{align}
The sum subtracts the part of the endpoint variance explained by the observed increments. For $T=1$ and equal subintervals, $K=1$ leaves approximately $0.03276$ residual variance; $K=4$ leaves approximately $0.00224$. These are analytic illustration values, not fluid-forecasting measurements. Refining nested features decreases expected residual variance by conditional-expectation projection. Our actual map uses increments of $M$ and a nonlinear learned drift, for which the residual is generally not available in closed form.
