\section{Experimental Study}
\subsection{Stochastic Navier--Stokes Forecasting}
Our scientific case uses 200 vorticity trajectories, each with 100 stored $128\times128$ fields. We retain the repository's 160/20/20 trajectory split and condition on the latest five fields. A recovered companion archive verifies a mean snapshot increment of 0.49989997016 in saved simulation-time units; original forcing covariance and physical coefficients remain unverified. Appendix~\ref{app:ns-details} gives normalization, provenance and detailed protocols.

Both maps initialize from their corresponding frozen teacher. Training uses 2,000 progressive-distillation updates followed by 500 same-path teacher-refinement updates; the latter uses 32 Euler-drift steps with exact integrated diffusion (Appendix~\ref{app:numerical-teacher}). Both stages are repeated with student seeds 0, 1 and 2. Validation alone selects checkpoints; the It\^o map uses four Brownian increments per transition unless the development ablation selects otherwise. All methods reuse datasets, conditioning and architecture family. Teacher pretraining cost is unavailable; map-training cost is recorded separately (Appendix~\ref{app:compute}).

One-interval testing uses three origins on each of 20 held-out trajectories, and autoregressive testing uses ten physical leads from a common origin on all 20 trajectories. Each forecast has 16 members. Fair CRPS is the primary score, together with energy score, spectral discrepancy and uncertainty diagnostics. Timings use synchronized, warmed-up sampling on one RTX 3090, including path-feature construction. Paired intervals resample trajectories rather than correlated grid cells and condition on the fixed models. Separate student-seed standard deviations describe only the three observed trainings.
