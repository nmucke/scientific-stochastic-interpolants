\section{Limitations and Discussion}
The forecasting distribution is constrained by history and training coverage. Compressed Brownian inputs, map error and teacher bias require separate diagnostics. Long autoregressive rollouts (Appendix~\ref{app:long-horizon}) test numerical boundedness and retained variability, but bounded trajectories do not establish distributional accuracy. \pending{long-horizon behavior of both maps relative to the matched solver references.}

The contribution is a conditional scientific-forecasting adaptation of existing map-learning principles, with a point-mass initial state and an evaluation of the quality--cost tradeoff. The planned study uses one stochastic-fluid archive, fixed teachers and three student trainings. Other consistency or adversarial-distillation baselines, independent teacher trainings, physical conditional reference ensembles and further dynamical systems remain necessary to assess generality. \pending{limitations specific to the new results.}
